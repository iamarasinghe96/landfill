ℹ What is the Diversion Rate?

Definition

The diversion rate is the percentage of total waste generated that is diverted from landfill through recycling, composting, or resource recovery.

How it's calculated

Diversion Rate = (Total Diverted ÷ Total Generated) × 100. This includes recycling, organics, e-waste, hazardous waste, and materials recovered at the transfer station.

Why it matters

Every tonne kept out of landfill reduces greenhouse gas emissions, saves resources, and helps AlburyCity meet state and local sustainability targets.

How you can help

Reduce recycling contamination, compost food scraps, use the e-waste drop-off, and participate in hard waste collections to boost our community's rate.
