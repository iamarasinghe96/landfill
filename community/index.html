Community Education Plan 2025-2030

AlburyCity's strategy to engage residents, reduce contamination, and drive diversion rates toward the NSW Government's 80% target by 2030.

Our Vision: Every resident knows their bin - and the city's diversion rate hits 70% by 2030

FOGO contamination is the single largest controllable factor in our diversion rate. An 18% recycling contamination rate means thousands of tonnes of recyclable material is lost to landfill each year. Through education, community empowerment, and real-time data, we can change this together.

Strategy 1

Digital Education Hub (This Page)

Provide residents with instant, searchable guidance on correct bin use via the Bin Finder tool, an interactive quiz, and downloadable resources. Embed on Council website and link from bin tags.

High Priority Live Now
🏫
Strategy 2

School Partnership Programme

Deliver Stage 2-4 curriculum-linked waste education sessions in all 22 Albury primary and secondary schools. Include "Bin Detective" class activities, school composting projects, and take-home challenges that engage the whole family.

High Priority Launching Term 2 2026
🤝
Strategy 3

Community Champions Network

Recruit and train 200 volunteer "Waste Champions" - one per street or apartment block. Champions receive regular contamination data for their street, share updates with neighbours, and help new residents understand the system. Recognise champions publicly each year.

High Priority Expressions of Interest Open
🏷️
Strategy 4

Bin Tag Education Programme

When collection crews identify a contaminated bin, attach a colour-coded tag explaining the problem and linking to this education hub. Data from tagging is fed back into the dashboard to identify contamination hotspots by street and suburb.

Ongoing High Impact
📱
Strategy 5

Social Media Campaign - #AlburyBins

Weekly "What Bin Wednesday" posts on Instagram, Facebook, and Nextdoor featuring common mistakes. Short-form video content showing correct bin use. Partnering with local food bloggers and community groups. Monthly giveaways for followers who share correct bin tips.

Ongoing Campaign Launching May 2026
🌍
Strategy 6

Multicultural Community Outreach

Partnering with settlement services, multicultural organisations, and CALD community leaders. Produce bin guides in Arabic, Italian, Hindi, Mandarin, Vietnamese, Punjabi, and Nepali. Deliver tailored workshops for new arrivals during community orientation sessions.

In Development Equity Focus
🏘️
Strategy 7

Apartment & High-Density Education

High-density residents have limited space and shared bins, creating unique contamination challenges. Deliver tailored bin guides for apartment buildings, work with body corporates and strata managers, and install clear visual guides in bin rooms. Host FOGO cooking demonstrations in common areas.

Pilot 2026
🏆
Strategy 8

Street Challenge - Lowest Contamination Wins

Annual competition where participating streets are measured on contamination rate over a 3-month period. Winning streets receive a community grant and public recognition. Quarterly suburb-level contamination data published on the main dashboard to encourage friendly competition.

Annual Event Community Engagement
